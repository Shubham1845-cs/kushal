## 📋 Quick Steps

### 1️⃣ Register New User
- Open `http://localhost:8085`
- Click **REGISTER** tab (top of form)
- Enter username and password (min 6 chars)
- Click **REGISTER** button (large, purple, bold)

### 2️⃣ Login
- Click **LOGIN** tab
- Enter your credentials
- Click **LOGIN** button (large, purple, bold)

### 3️⃣ View Dashboard
- See your username
- View all registered users
- See encrypted passwords (BCrypt hashes)
